// how it works

How the check works

The same data you see in the app, checked against the source, on a schedule nobody touches.

⏱

Hourly, automatic

At 7 minutes past every hour, Pass Checker reads each region's official feed fresh and compares it with what the app was serving at that exact moment.

📋

Every miss is listed

If the app and the DOT disagree, it is a mismatch and it appears on this page with the date, the pass, and both statuses. No exceptions.

🔄

Refreshed every 10 minutes

Pass data in these regions refreshes every 10 minutes, so most mismatches are a road that changed status in the minutes before a check.

This report covers the seven regions whose feeds Pass Checker reads directly: Washington (WSDOT), British Columbia (DriveBC), Colorado (CDOT), California (Caltrans), Utah (UDOT), Idaho (Idaho 511) and Arizona (AZ511). Other regions are not included. Pass Checker can only be as current as the DOT itself, so always confirm with the official source before you travel.
