The short version

Pass Checker is the fastest way to answer one question: is the pass open? Live status, DOT cameras, and summit weather for 134 mountain passes, pulled straight from official sources, with none of the ads or scrolling of a DOT website.

Story angles

  • The seasonal utility. Every first storm, hundreds of thousands of drivers search "is Snoqualmie Pass open." Pass Checker answers it in one tap, with the camera feed to prove it.
  • One person, whole product. Design, native code, backend infrastructure, and data integrations for 32 states and provinces, shipped by a one person Washington studio on a real release calendar.
  • Privacy as a feature. In a category full of location trackers and ad networks, Pass Checker collects nothing. The App Store privacy label is empty by design.
  • The terminal themes. Six phosphor CRT themes (green, amber, blue, pink, plum, white) with scanlines and a blinking cursor. A weather utility with an actual point of view on design.
