Mountain passes that close for winter

18 seasonal closures · typical dates · live status on each pass page

Most mountain passes are plowed and stay open all winter. These 18 are not: each one is gated when the snow arrives and stays shut until spring. The windows below are typical patterns from past seasons. The agency that maintains each road sets the actual dates every year, so open a pass page for its live status.

North Cascades SR 20, Washington

Typically closes: mid-November to early December
Typically reopens: April or May

WSDOT closes the highway between Ross Dam Trailhead and Silver Star gate once avalanche chutes above the road load with snow.

Cayuse Pass SR 410, Washington

Typically closes: mid-November to early December
Typically reopens: April or May

Cayuse usually closes within days of Chinook Pass and reopens a few weeks ahead of it.

Chinook Pass SR 410, Washington

Typically closes: mid-November
Typically reopens: late May, usually by Memorial Day weekend

WSDOT closes the pass at Crystal Mountain Boulevard on the west side and Morse Creek on the east side.

McKenzie Pass OR 242, Oregon

Typically closes: early November, or sooner after the first heavy snow
Typically reopens: mid-to-late June

The narrow lava-field highway is not plowed in winter. Vehicles over 35 feet are prohibited all year.

Tioga Pass CA 120, California

Typically closes: November, with the first significant snowfall
Typically reopens: late May or June, later after heavy winters

The closure covers Tioga Road through Yosemite National Park, from Crane Flat to the Tioga Pass entrance.

Monitor Pass CA 89, California

Typically closes: November or December
Typically reopens: April, often the first Sierra pass to reopen

Monitor closes later and reopens earlier than Sonora, Ebbetts, and Tioga.

Sonora Pass CA 108, California

Typically closes: November or December
Typically reopens: May

Caltrans closes the pass east of Strawberry once snow makes the steep upper grades unsafe.

Ebbetts Pass CA 4, California

Typically closes: November or December
Typically reopens: May or June

The closure runs from Lake Alpine to the junction with CA 89.

Independence Pass CO 82, Colorado

Typically closes: early to mid November, or earlier if heavy snow arrives
Typically reopens: the Thursday before Memorial Day

CDOT gates the highway east of Aspen and west of Twin Lakes. Vehicles over 35 feet are prohibited all year.

Trail Ridge Road US 34, Colorado

Typically closes: mid-to-late October
Typically reopens: late May, around Memorial Day weekend

Rocky Mountain National Park closes the road between Many Parks Curve and the Colorado River Trailhead.

Bald Mountain Pass UT 150, Utah

Typically closes: November
Typically reopens: late May, around Memorial Day

The Mirror Lake Highway is not plowed over the summit in winter and becomes a snowmobile route.

Monte Cristo Summit UT 39, Utah

Typically closes: late November or December
Typically reopens: May

UDOT closes UT 39 over the summit between the Ogden Valley side and Woodruff.

Hatcher Pass AK 1, Alaska

Typically closes: late September or October, with the first snow
Typically reopens: around July 1

The gravel road over the summit closes. The paved road to Independence Mine on the Palmer side stays open in winter.

Smugglers Notch VT 108, Vermont

Typically closes: mid-October to mid-November, depending on snow
Typically reopens: mid-May

The Notch section between Stowe and Jeffersonville is not plowed. Tractor trailers are prohibited all year.

Hazens Notch VT 58, Vermont

Typically closes: November
Typically reopens: May

The unpaved Notch section between Montgomery Center and Lowell is not maintained in winter.

Evans Notch ME 113, Maine

Typically closes: November
Typically reopens: mid-May

The road through the White Mountain National Forest is gated and unplowed in winter.

Needles Highway SD 87, South Dakota

Typically closes: with the first snow, usually by November
Typically reopens: around April 1

Custer State Park closes the highway and its narrow tunnels for the winter.

Highwood Pass Hwy 40, Alberta

Typically closes: December 1
Typically reopens: June 15

This closure is fixed by date every year to protect wildlife winter range, regardless of snow.
