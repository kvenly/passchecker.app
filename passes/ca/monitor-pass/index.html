When does Monitor Pass close for the winter?

Monitor Pass closes every winter. Typical closing: November or December. Typical reopening: April, often the first Sierra pass to reopen. Monitor closes later and reopens earlier than Sonora, Ebbetts, and Tioga. Exact dates change every year, so check the live status before you go.
